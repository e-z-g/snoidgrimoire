SnoidGrimoire

Reads the files of The Logical Journey of the Zoombinis (Brøderbund, 1996): every picture, sprite sheet, sound, tune, palette, cursor, string, walk and animation script in its Mohawk archives.

Drop the game's .MHK archives, its DATA folder or its CD image here
  • The archives are in DATA on the game's CD, 22 of them, BASECAMP.MHK to ZOOMBINI.MHK.
  • A CD image (.iso) is read as it is: the archives are taken out of its DATA folder.
  • Nothing leaves your browser.

Or read the copy the Internet Archive hosts: the Learning Company's re-release of 2001, whose archives differ from 1996's in places. Each archive is fetched from archive.org when you open it, 0.5 to 24 MB.
